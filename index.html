Amune Clinic Autopilot

Fewer no-shows, fuller chairs and less paperwork, so doctors and front desk can focus on patients.

For outpatient clinics, polyclinics and dental practices with 1 to 20 practitioners whose front desk is overloaded and whose clinicians finish notes after hours.

No-showsEmpty chairs that nobody had time to refill.
Lapsed recallsSix-month check-ups and cleanings that never get rebooked.

What we automate, across the patient journey

  1. 01

    24/7 AI receptionist

    Answers WhatsApp, web chat and calls in Arabic and English. Books, reschedules and cancels directly in your practice management system, and hands complex cases to staff.

  2. 02

    Pre-visit intake

    Digital forms for medical history, consent and insurance details sent before the visit and filed to the patient record.

  3. 03

    Reminders & waitlist refill

    Smart reminders with one-tap confirm. Cancelled slots offered automatically to patients on the waitlist.

  4. 04

    Clinical note drafting

    AI scribe drafts SOAP or dental charting notes from the consultation for the clinician to review and sign. Nothing is filed without clinician approval.

  5. 05

    Insurance & claims prep

    Pre-approval requests and claim documents assembled from the note, checked against payer requirements before submission.

  6. 06

    Recall, treatment follow-up & reviews

    Patients due for hygiene, check-ups or pending treatment plans are contacted automatically. Happy patients get a review request.
